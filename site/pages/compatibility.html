PSVR2 Linux compatibility

"Tested" means someone ran it and sent logs. "Expected" means it should work, but nobody has reported it yet. Reports are welcome.

Hardware

  • PlayStation VR2 headset with Sony's PS VR2 PC adapter (required).
  • PS VR2 Sense controllers over Bluetooth.

GPUs

GPUStatusNotes
NVIDIATestedRTX 5080, proprietary driver.
AMDExpectedNo session reported yet.
Intel ArcRuns, with limitsA750 (xe and i915): no async reprojection; Room View can crash SteamVR's compositor.
