PSVR2 passthrough on Linux

PSVR2-DriverKit gives SteamVR the headset's cameras, so Room View shows your surroundings.

What you get Stable

  • Room View: press the headset's function button (under the visor) or use the SteamVR dashboard.
  • The wall camera: the camera picture appears near your play-area boundary.
  • Camera during room setup.

Turn it on once: DriverKit → Compile Driver → Turn on PS VR2 features (SteamVR closed). Undo restores your previous SteamVR settings.

Settings

  • Headset cameras in SteamVR: on, noimages or off.
  • Lighter camera picture: half size for PCs where Room View stutters.

Limitations

  • SteamVR only; there's no passthrough on Monado.
  • On Intel Arc, SteamVR's compositor can crash building the camera view (a Mesa issue): set the cameras to off.
  • The picture comes from the headset's black-and-white tracking cameras.
