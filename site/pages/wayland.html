PSVR2 on Wayland and X11

SteamVR and Monado take over the headset's display by leasing it from your desktop. On Wayland your compositor has to support DRM leasing (wp_drm_lease_device_v1).

Your desktop inside VR Works

SteamVR's own Desktop panel stays blank on Wayland. That's a SteamVR issue, not something this driver can fix. PSVR2-DriverKit works with WayVR, a separate open-source overlay for Wayland and X11 screens:

  1. Install WayVR (Arch AUR wayvr, or its AppImage in ~/Applications).
  2. DriverKit → Settings → Start WayVR with SteamVR.
  3. Allow the screen-share prompt the first time.

The driver includes Sense bindings for it: double-press Triangle to show or hide, triggers to click, L1/R1 to grab, sticks to scroll. Close SteamVR's dashboard (PS button) first.

Works with SteamVR or Monado. With Monado: Settings → Start WayVR with monado-service.
