The PSVR2-DriverKit app

  • Compile Driver: installs packages and udev rules, builds the driver, registers it with Steam, and turns on SteamVR's camera features.
  • Launcher: starts SteamVR or monado-service with pre-flight checks and log collection; sets the play area.
  • Settings: render size, refresh rate, controller layouts for older games, cameras, hand tracking, WayVR.
  • Device status: headset and controllers.

Planned: per-user eye calibration and a built-in hidden-area mask. See the roadmap.
