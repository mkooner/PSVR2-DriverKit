PSVR2 Sense controllers on Linux

Full 6DoF tracking, every input and haptics, in SteamVR and in OpenXR apps.

Pairing

  1. Hold Create + PS until the light flashes.
  2. Pair it in your Bluetooth settings.
  3. Repeat with the other controller. Later, pressing PS reconnects it.

Tracking Stable

The headset's cameras see the controllers' LEDs; the driver identifies each one by its blink pattern and fuses the camera positions with the controllers' motion sensors. Hold the controllers in view for about 5 seconds after starting. Very bright sunlight interrupts tracking.

Inputs and haptics Stable

  • Cross, Circle, Square, Triangle, Create, Options, PS; sticks with click; L1/R1; L2/R2.
  • Capacitive touch on triggers, grips, sticks and face buttons; finger-distance sensors.
  • Vibration.
  • A finger skeleton for SteamVR games that animate hands.

Bindings

SteamVR gets PS VR2 input profiles; the PS button opens the dashboard. Games without SteamVR Input see Oculus Touch controllers by default; DriverKit → Settings switches to Vive, Index or the native PS VR2 layout.
