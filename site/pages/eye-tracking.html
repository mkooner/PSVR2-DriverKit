PSVR2 eye tracking on Linux

PSVR2-DriverKit passes the headset's built-in eye tracking on to SteamVR and OpenXR apps. Nothing to set up: it's on whenever the driver runs.

RuntimeInterfaceStatus
SteamVRSteamVR's eye-tracking input on the headsetWorks
OpenXR (Monado)XR_EXT_eye_gaze_interactionWorks

Limitations

  • No per-user calibration yet. Gaze comes from the headset's own estimate and can be a few degrees off for some people. Calibration is planned.
  • Only games and apps that read eye-gaze input use it.
